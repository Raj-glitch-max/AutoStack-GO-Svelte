**What You Do:**
1. Click a button

3. See the price BEFORE you pay
4. Click "Deploy"
5. Done! Your app is live

**Normally this takes:** 2-4 weeks and costs $120,000/year for a DevOps engineer  
**With AutoStack:** 5 minutes and costs $5-500/month for cloud resources
